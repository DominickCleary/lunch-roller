# Gambling about lunch rules

These rules are implemented by the local MVP. Optional notification delivery requires VAPID configuration and a supported browser; production HTTPS/device verification remains an environment check.

One workplace shares an administrator-managed roster and wheel. Participants pick a name and the browser remembers it, without accounts or passwords. Anyone can impersonate a roster member; this is an accepted workplace trust model. Name selection never authorizes administration.

A fresh workplace starts without people or places. An administrator must add at least one active person and place and finish setup before play or scheduled draws begin. Visitors must choose a name; a valid saved browser selection enters automatically and can be changed.

## Daily draw

- Default schedule: Monday–Friday, 11:30, Europe/Dublin. Admin may change time, weekdays, and IANA timezone. ISO weekday numbers are 1=Monday through 7=Sunday.
- At most one result for a local calendar date. No manual rerolls. Schedule changes do not replace today's result.
- Each active option has a positive conceptual weight, stored as its natural logarithm. Seed weight is 1 (log 0). Odds are weight divided by the sum of active weights.
- Selection uses server cryptographic uniform randomness. Persist the random sample, pre-draw odds/weights/name snapshot, selected name, date, and timezone, then subtract ln(2) from the winner's log weight.
- Each place is rated Treat, Balanced (default), or Healthy when created; admins can edit ratings. After a Treat place wins, active Healthy places receive a permanent weight multiplier (default ×1.25), in addition to the winner’s usual halving. Balanced/Healthy wins trigger no bonus. Admins can disable bonuses or set the multiplier greater than 1 and at most 100. Ratings, affected names, multiplier, and before/after bonus chances are saved with the draw; edits never rewrite history. Bonus chances compare the post-halving distribution before versus after the bonus. No tokens are spent or awarded.
- The animation presents the committed winner. Each draw supplies a deterministic versioned plan derived from its saved sample, ID, snapshot and timestamp: a saved admin-configured duration (5–60 seconds, default 20), several fast turns, a slow cubic deceleration and a landing point inside the winning slice with a small boundary inset. A three-second lead-in allows visible browsers to fetch the same plan. Live playback uses the server clock; late loads and returning tabs catch up. Replays start at zero and repeat that plan locally without drawing again. The completed spin opens a winner modal with confetti and holds its saved wheel/landing until dismissal. Reduced motion skips rotation and confetti. Winner text is withheld during the normal live reveal; the committed result remains available through APIs and other game records, so this is presentation rather than secrecy.
- Decay persists indefinitely. No resets or floors. Relative weights use max-subtracted exponentials for numerical stability; probabilities below floating-point resolution may display as zero.
- The worker checks every 30 seconds. A late tick processes today's due draw only. Never backfill missed dates. A weekend/non-scheduled day has no draw. No active places means no result; adding an option permits a retry that day.
- Admins can mark the next scheduled roll to be skipped for an event. The skip is tied to its local draw date, so a delayed worker will not draw it later. Open bets and staged influence for that date are refunded when the skip is set; the wheel and next betting market move to the following scheduled roll. A pending skip can be cancelled before its scheduled time. Changing the schedule clears it.
- The wheel always shows live odds for the next scheduled draw. Once today’s draw commits, its target advances immediately (Friday to Monday on a weekday schedule). The live animation and winner dialog temporarily hold the saved result; dismissing the dialog returns to live odds. Previous results do not remain on the wheel or in a persistent winner banner. Replay and check-in remain available. Pending draws stay the target until committed; skipped draws advance to the next scheduled date.

## Tokens

- After a draw commits, member boosts/reductions, bet edits and paid restaurant additions/removals open immediately for the next scheduled draw by default. Admins can set a post-roll delay of 0–1440 minutes from the committed draw timestamp (0 is immediate). A delay continues across midnight and uses elapsed minutes, including across daylight-saving changes. Check-in and transfers keep their existing timing. Activity groups purchases by target spin date, including across weekends; older unlinked purchases have inferred attribution without becoming editable.

- Participants can immediately send 1–2,147,483,647 available tokens to another active participant, without a fee. Transfers are final, have no betting cutoff, cannot target yourself, and append a matching debit/credit pair with a shared transfer ID, counterparties and saved names. Both sides commit together under the settings lock; retries cannot double-send and concurrent spending cannot overdraw. Reserved tokens must first be released through their normal controls before they can be sent. Transfers use the existing trust-based identity model.
- Initial balance is zero. Balance is the sum of immutable ledger entries.
- One self check-in earns the configured participation reward (default 10 tokens) for that day's result, from the draw until local midnight, using the timezone saved on the draw. Changing settings must not move an existing attendance deadline.
- Check-in displays the admin-editable pledge (default “I pledge that I actually obeyed the wheel”). A confirmed check-in becomes grey and disabled for that person/draw, including after reload or in another tab. The pledge is an assertion shown to the participant; the server's one-reward attendance rule remains authoritative.
- Stage a boost/reduction unit at the configured price (default 5 each) to multiply an active place's relative weight by the configured factor (defaults 1.25 and 0.8). Live odds include staged units. Until the betting market's saved cutoff, add units or withdraw your own units for that draw. Withdrawal reverses the unit's original logarithmic effect and refunds its original price, even if economy settings have changed. Units are withdrawn newest first within a person's chosen restaurant and direction. Each person has one signed position per restaurant and draw: adding a boost cancels their newest reduction first, and adding a reduction cancels their newest boost first. Cancellation refunds that unit’s original cost instead of charging for an opposite unit. Older mixed positions are paired and refunded on the next adjustment before cutoff. Different people can oppose each other. A single restaurant refund returns all your pending units without changing your bet. The server enforces the exact cutoff for additions and withdrawals, including while a scheduled draw is delayed. A draw commits the effects permanently; the next draw starts with zero new units while retaining the existing weights. Purchases predating this feature have no editable units and remain historical.
- Spend the configured new-place price (default 20) to add an active place immediately. Starting weight is the arithmetic mean of current active weights, calculated in log space, multiplied by the admin-configured new-place factor (default 1); use 1 as the average when the wheel is empty. Re-adding a restaurant name that was removed restores its archived record, resets it to this starting weight, and charges the same add-place price.
- Spend the configured remove-place price (default 40) to banish an active restaurant from the wheel immediately. Additions and banishments are final purchases with no user undo/refund control. Banishing a restaurant refunds all pending boost/reduction units and open bet stakes on that restaurant for every participant, even after cutoff if the draw has not committed. Other restaurants' positions remain. The restaurant and historical references are archived, not deleted. It can be added again as a new paid action at its configured starting weight.
- Admin can edit these eight values under Tokens and odds, including the removal price and new-place factor. Token values must be positive whole numbers; boost factors are greater than 1 up to 100 and reduce factors range from 0.01 to below 1; the new-place factor ranges from 0.01 to 100. Settings apply to future actions; historical ledger entries and idempotent retries retain their original amounts and effects. Winner decay remains one half after each draw.
- Admin can set an exact participant token balance with a required reason. The server appends the difference to that person's ledger; prior entries are never rewritten. Admin can equalize a selected subset by setting each selected restaurant to the overall average `1/N` chance; unselected restaurants scale proportionally to fill the remainder. Admin can also set all restaurants equal or edit one restaurant's percentage, scaling the rest proportionally. The distribution remains 100%. Percentage edits preserve the total active base weight after refunding pending influence, allocating that total according to the requested percentages; saving unchanged percentages does not reset the weight scale. Saved results are unchanged. Participants can toggle the odds list between chance percentages and fair fractional odds (`(1-p)/p`); these theoretical odds are not a payout.
- Participants can place one token-only bet on a restaurant for the next scheduled draw using a whole-token stake up to their balance. The stake is reserved immediately. They can change the restaurant or stake, or cancel and get the stake back, until the configurable cutoff (default 5 minutes before the draw; admin range 0–1440). Each market snapshots its cutoff when created.
- Current fair fractional odds are `(1 - p) / p : 1`, where `p` is the restaurant's current chance. Expected gross return is `floor(stake / p)` and net gain is gross return minus stake. These estimates change while odds remain live. Settlement uses the actual draw's saved pre-decay probability snapshot; a winning bet receives that gross return and a losing bet receives nothing. Gross return is capped at 2,147,483,647 tokens per bet because ledger entries use signed 32-bit integers.
- Schedule edits refund open bets and pending influence before changing the schedule. If a market expires without a draw, its stakes and influence are refunded when the next market is requested; missed dates are never drawn retroactively. Refunds reverse the saved influence effects. Admin edits to exact restaurant odds first refund pending influence before applying the new distribution.
- Disabling or archiving a participant refunds their pending influence and open stakes, while retaining their history.
- No overdrafts. Repeated requests cannot double-charge or double-reward. Admin corrections append a signed delta with a reason, also without making the balance negative.
- Names are trimmed, 1–100 characters, and case-insensitively unique among active records. Blank or already-active restaurant additions are rejected without charging. Removing a restaurant archives it and preserves history; adding that name later restores the same record with its configured starting weight.
- Admins may remove roster members. Unused profiles are permanently deleted; profiles referenced by attendance, ledger entries, added places or subscriptions are archived, with history retained and notifications retired.

## Notifications

Ask for Web Push permission only after a user clicks an opt-in control. Store subscriptions per browser/participant. Production needs HTTPS and VAPID configuration; support and delivery depend on the browser/device. Missing permission does not prevent play. Alerts deliver committed draw results and notify recipients of token transfers and winning bet payouts. Check-in rewards do not trigger alerts. Push failure never rerolls or reverses game state. Browser animation presents the committed winner.

Admin weight overrides accept a finite positive relative weight and store its logarithm. Like percentage overrides, they first refund pending influence; other base weights and historical draw snapshots stay intact. Spin duration is saved on each new draw, so later preference edits cannot alter its shared animation. Legacy draws retain their original deterministic 18–21 second plan.
